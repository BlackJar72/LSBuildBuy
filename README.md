# Build-Buy Prototype (early, never to be finished)

## Do to the flurry of new age verification laws I do not have the infrastructure to comply with, I will not be making or working on videogames or any other standalone software applications. ##

Part of an abandoned Life Simulation Game project, which was hoped (with varying degrees of modification) could be adapted for use with other types of 
games such as RPGs or (in a more simplified and streamlined form) city builders.

[Short Youtube Video](https://youtu.be/nVQr_RHpcBo?si=L0dYcttYknKMhjfV)

Basically, I did some "soul searching" and realized I didn't want to spend the next decade, or even the next few years, of my life working on a Sims 
clone that, while possibly better to my own taste in a few specific ways would just have a lot less to and not as good over all.

The other direction I was working on this from (and having a lot more trouble with) was the build-buy mode, found here:

https://github.com/BlackJar72/CharacterEngine

I doubt I'll ever get anything out of it either way, and its likely no one will even look at it so that it will influence anything, but after all the work 
I did put into it I'd rather make it publically available so it might have some chance of not all going completely to waste.

This portion of the project was never going well.  It was trouble with this that led to a long pause in development for half a year before starting again 
and getting this far.  I doubt it would ever have become a good system.  I doubt I'd ever have non-axis-aligned walls, a way to detect enclosed rooms 
without using room-create tool to make it all at once, or to have non-rectangular rooms (that would be recognized as one room by game logic).
